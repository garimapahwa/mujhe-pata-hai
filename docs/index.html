Mujhe Pata Hai

A voice buddy that lives in your Mac's menu bar. When you slack off, it notices, scolds you in Hinglish, listens to your excuse and answers back.

Voices

MummyStrict, dramatic, loving
DostBest friend who roasts you
CommentatorLive cricket commentary of your slacking
Filmy NarratorDeep, dramatic baritone
Action HeroLoud, table-thumping hero

Options in the menu

On / OffSwitch at the top of the menu
VolumeSlider, soft to loud
Demo modeCatches you after 8 seconds instead of 7 minutes
Show buddySmall animated character on your screen
Check my screenNotices YouTube, Netflix, Instagram and catches the lie
Launch at loginStarts with your Mac

Install

  1. Get a free API key at dashboard.sarvam.ai.
  2. Open Terminal, paste this and press Return:
    curl -fsSL https://garimapahwa.github.io/mujhe-pata-hai/install.sh | bash
    Paste your key when it asks.
  3. Click the 👁 in your menu bar, choose a voice and turn it on.
  4. Allow the microphone when macOS asks.
Requires macOS 13 or later. Voice by Sarvam AI. Source on GitHub.
To uninstall: curl -fsSL https://garimapahwa.github.io/mujhe-pata-hai/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall
