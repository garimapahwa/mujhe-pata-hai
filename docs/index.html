मुझे पता है · Mujhe Pata Hai

A voice buddy in your Mac's menu bar. Slack off and she catches you, in Hinglish.

curl -fsSL https://garimapahwa.github.io/mujhe-pata-hai/install.sh | bash

Paste in Terminal. macOS 13+, no admin password. You'll need a free Sarvam AI key.

  1. An 👁 appears in your menu bar. Pick a character and flip the switch.
  2. Go idle or open YouTube and she scolds you out loud. It gets louder if you ignore her.
  3. Answer her. She listens, checks your screen, and catches the lie.
  4. Get back to work and she stops mid-sentence. “आ गई? Good.”
